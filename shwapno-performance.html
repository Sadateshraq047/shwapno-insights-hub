Reading guide

What the columns mean

The Business Performance tables compare this year with the mapped same day last year.

SALE

Sale This / Sale Last

Net sales value (NSI) for this year and for the matching days last year. Diff and Growth % show the gap between the two.

FF

Footfall

Number of distinct invoices. It counts bills, not people, so one customer with two bills counts twice.

BS

Basket size

Sale value divided by footfall: how much an average bill is worth.

GP%

Gross profit margin

Profit divided by NSI from the SAP billing data. The GP% diff card shows the change in points against last year.

GPV

Gross profit value

GP% multiplied by the invoice sales value, so the margin is applied to the same sales base the Sale columns use.

GRT%

Growth %

This year minus last year, divided by last year. A negative figure means the measure fell against the same days last year.
